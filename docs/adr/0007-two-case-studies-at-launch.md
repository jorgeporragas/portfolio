# ADR-007 — Two case studies at launch
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-23

Context: The PRD planned three or four development case studies at launch, sticky.md among
them. The content inventory lists three candidates: sticky.md, this site and Banana Splits.
On 2026-10-23 Jorge confirmed the Banana Splits client agreed to showing it, but deferred the
case study: the app and its layout are still changing, so screenshots and text would date
quickly. meedee was dropped earlier because it was never finished. PRD veto 5 rules out
filling the gap with placeholder work.

Decision: Launch with two development case studies, sticky.md and this site. Banana Splits
joins when the app settles, before or after launch (#33).

Consequences: PRD › Content at launch says two. Adding a case study later is a content pull
request, not a change of scope.
